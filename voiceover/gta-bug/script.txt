The biggest crime game on Earth only exists because the police were broken.

In nineteen ninety-five, in Dundee, Scotland, a small studio called DMA Design started a new game. The same team that made Lemmings.

It was called Race and Chase. A simple driving game about cops and robbers.

But it was not going well. It was way behind schedule. And the company paying for it wanted to cancel it.

Then something weird happened.

The police cars went crazy. Instead of pulling you over, they rammed you. They hunted you through the streets, smashing into anything to stop you.

It was a bug. The code that told the police where to drive was broken.

You see, the team should have fixed it. But when people played it, being chased by mad cops was the most fun part of the whole game.

So they kept the bug. They threw out the boring rules. And they made you the bad guy.

They gave the game a new name. Grand Theft Auto.

It came out in nineteen ninety-seven. Today, the series has sold over four hundred and seventy million games.

So next time a GTA cop rams you off the road, don't get mad. He's not broken. He's the reason the game exists.
